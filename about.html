Our story

Built around the fire

Since 2019

A neighbourhood kitchen with coastal roots.

The Kitchen began as a conversation between two friends who grew up between Lisbon and the Algarve - one drawn to the grill, the other to the dining room. We wanted a place that felt like someone’s home kitchen scaled up: generous portions, honest flavours, and a room where the evening could stretch.

Today we cook Portuguese-Mediterranean food with a light hand - olive oil, wood smoke, citrus, and herbs. We buy from the morning markets in Ribeira, fish from Cascais boats when the weather allows, and wine from small producers who still label bottles by hand.

What guides us

Three things we never compromise

01

Season first

The menu bends to what is good right now. When tomatoes are at their peak, they take the spotlight. When the sea is rough, we lean inland.

02

Fire as flavour

Our wood oven and open grill are not decoration. Smoke, char, and gentle heat shape almost everything that leaves the pass.

03

Warm service

We remember regulars, pace the meal with you, and treat first visits like introductions - never transactions.

The people

In the kitchen and on the floor

A small team, a shared table culture, and a belief that hospitality is a craft of its own.

Inês Carvalho

Chef & Co-founder

Inês trained in Lisbon and spent formative years cooking on the Costa Vicentina. She leads the menu with a focus on seafood, vegetables, and fire - always asking how little we can do to an ingredient and still make it sing.

Miguel Duarte

General Manager & Co-founder

Miguel shapes the wine list and the rhythm of the room. He has worked dining rooms from Porto to Barcelona, and believes the best meals feel both special and completely at ease.
