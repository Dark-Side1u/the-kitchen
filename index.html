Our approach

Cooked simply. Seasoned with the coast.

We work from the Atlantic and the hills - day’s catch, market vegetables, olive oil pressed nearby, and bread baked before the doors open.

The menu shifts with the week. Some plates stay; others appear once, when the ingredient asks for them. Come hungry, stay a while, and leave the evening unhurried.
